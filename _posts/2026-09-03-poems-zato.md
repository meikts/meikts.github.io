> A black day splattered
> 
> On the parquet, on the tiles.
> 
> The roused mass
> 
> Soils its hands in darkness.
> 
>
> Matches strike at the phosphorus,
> 
> Smoke lays in my palm.
> 
> The smell of gas is burning out
> 
> And fire crackles in my ears.
> 
>
> It’s so warm, bright and sweet,
> 
> Even as a chill runs down my skin.
> 
> It’s a wholesome painting,
> 
> Even if it hangs askew.

白日昏沉，弄了四處，

蓋住磚瓦、深入黑土。

陰影中，巨人被喚醒，

張牙舞爪、沾滿泥濘。


劃過紅磷點一根火柴，

掌心裏漸漸落定霧靄。

火焰在耳中不斷爆裂，

又怎堪一聞灰飛煙滅？


你溫暖、明亮、甜美，

一陣寒戰能有何所謂？

若是畫兒會給人安慰，

無妨裝裱得顫顫巍巍。


--- 

> O damp black earth, 
> 
> Do not hold me so tenderly, 
> 
> Do not tear your snow-white attire, 
> 
> And sleep peacefully. 
> 
> 
> I will scratch and I will beat, 
> 
> But frost will still glimmer, 
> 
> And birds will still fly across the sky, 
> 
> And a pale dawn will still shine.

噯，我潮溼的黑土，

不要將我優柔困住，

也不要使白衣玷污，

睡去吧，安然閉目。


我要劃破、要痛打，

卻無損晶瑩的霜花，

鳥兒飛過未曾落下，

總不免黯淡的朝霞。
